# Nexus — Workforce & Performance Intelligence System

## 1. Problem Statement
Modern organizations face significant challenges in accurately evaluating employee performance, tracking customer interaction outcomes, and aligning daily operations with strategic goals. Traditional management processes are often manual, biased, and reactive, leading to operational inefficiencies and unaddressed workforce risks.

## 2. Solution Overview
Nexus is an AI-powered Workforce Intelligence Platform designed to bridge the gap between executive management and operational teams. It automates performance evaluations, tracks client interactions, aligns individual tasks with organizational KPIs, and provides actionable insights to executives in real time.

## 3. Type of AI Used
* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** For interactive administrative querying, prompt handling, and intent parsing.
* **Algorithmic Evaluation Engine:** For automated, unbiased performance scoring based on operational metrics and client interaction logs.
* **Automated Data Analytics:** For real-time risk identification, KPI tracking, and trend analysis.

## 4. Challenge Topic
**Wild Card Challenge (Future of Work):** Intelligent systems for workforce management, decision-making support, and operational automation across sectors.

## 5. How IBM Bob / IBM Technology was Used
IBM Bob and IBM SkillsBuild AI concepts were utilized to guide the architectural design of the intelligent decision-making logic, prompt engineering methodologies, and structured data-handling routines embedded within the Nexus analytical engine.

---

## Key Features
* **AI Executive Assistant:** Query team performance, costs, and risk factors using natural language.
* **Automated Performance Analytics:** Objective strength and growth-area identification without manual bias.
* **Customer Interaction Logging:** Real-time tracking and classification of client cases (Resolved, Follow-up, Escalated, At-Risk).
* **KPI & Goal Mapping:** Direct linkage of daily deliverables to high-level strategic OKRs.
* **Multi-Language & Modern UI:** Dual-language (Arabic/English - RTL/LTR) support with a responsive dark-mode interface.
